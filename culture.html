Historical development

Roman Culture changed substantially between the Republic, Principate, third-century crisis, late Empire and eastern Roman periods. A useful encyclopedia must preserve those chronological differences rather than flatten them.

Regional variation

Rome governed a diverse world. Practices in Italy, Gaul, Egypt, Syria, Greece, Africa, Britain and Anatolia were not identical, and local institutions often survived inside Roman structures.

Evidence

Inscriptions, papyri, coins, buildings, archaeological remains and literary sources provide complementary evidence. No single ancient author can stand for the whole empire.
