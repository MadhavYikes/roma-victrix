Sources & Bibliography

Roma Aeterna is designed as a reference work, not an unsourced blog. The article system separates interpretation from primary evidence and points users toward institutional and scholarly resources.

De Imperatoribus Romanis

The peer-reviewed DIR project describes itself as an online encyclopedia of Roman rulers and families, with imperial indexes, biographies, family trees, battle indexes and maps. It covers the Roman imperial tradition into the Byzantine period.

Open De Imperatoribus Romanis →

British Museum

The British Museum's Roman material includes coins, sculpture and educational resources. Its Roman coin material specifically discusses how imperial coinage communicated rulers' images and political messages.

British Museum Collection →

LacusCurtius / University of Chicago

LacusCurtius provides digital editions and translations of ancient and historical texts, including Strabo, Roman histories and reference works. These are valuable for primary-source reading and older scholarly reference material.

LacusCurtius Roman materials →
