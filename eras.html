Eras of Roman History

From the Republic to 1453. The chronology is divided into useful historical periods without pretending that the transitions were instantaneous.

133–27 BC

Roman Republic to Principate

From the late Republic crisis and civil wars to Augustus.

27 BC–68 AD

Julio-Claudian Rome

The first imperial dynasty.

69–96 AD

Flavian Rome

Civil-war recovery, the Colosseum and the Flavians.

96–192 AD

High Empire

Adoptive emperors, expansion and consolidation.

235–284 AD

Crisis of the Third Century

Military emperors, invasions and breakaway states.

284–395 AD

Tetrarchy and Constantinian Age

Diocletian, Constantine and the transformation of empire.

395–476 AD

Late Western Empire

The final western court and the transition to post-Roman kingdoms.

395–1453 AD

Eastern Roman Empire

The Roman state continued from Constantinople for another millennium.
