Denomination and metrology

A common lower denomination of the imperial monetary system.

Obverse

Roman imperial obverses commonly carried the ruler’s portrait with titulature identifying office, names, honors and victories.

Reverse

Reverse types could show gods, personifications, military standards, buildings, ceremonies, victories, family members and political ideals.

Mints

Coins were produced at multiple mints. Mint systems changed over time as emperors reorganized the monetary and military infrastructure.

Propaganda

Coinage was a mass medium. Its small format allowed imperial claims to circulate with ordinary transactions throughout the empire.

Collecting and identification

Collectors identify coins through denomination, emperor, portrait, legend, reverse type, mint mark, date range and die characteristics. Authentication requires specialist expertise.
