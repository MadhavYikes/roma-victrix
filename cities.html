Cities of the Roman World

Urban civilization. Cities were the basic nodes through which Roman government, religion, trade and public life operated.

Italy

Rome

Capital, political center and symbolic heart of the empire.

Thrace

Constantinople

Eastern imperial capital from Constantine onward.

Egypt

Alexandria

Major port, intellectual center and administrative capital of Egypt.

Syria

Antioch

Major eastern metropolis and military-diplomatic center.

Africa

Carthage

Great North African city rebuilt under Roman rule and later capital of the Vandal kingdom.

Asia

Ephesus

Major Anatolian port and civic center.

Britannia

Londinium

Major Roman settlement and commercial center in Britain.

Gaul

Lugdunum

Major Gallic administrative and military center.

Italy

Pompeii

Vesuvian city preserved by the eruption of 79 AD.

Italy

Ravenna

Late western imperial capital and major Adriatic port.

Gaul

Trier

Major late Roman imperial residence and military center.

Bithynia

Nicomedia

Important imperial residence in the Tetrarchic period.

Pannonia

Sirmium

Major Danubian city and imperial residence.

Judea

Jerusalem

Major religious center repeatedly reshaped by Roman rule.
