Roman Timeline

From the late Republic to 1453. The chronology connects rulers, wars, institutions and turning points.

133 BC

Gracchi and the late Republican crisis

The period of civil conflict and constitutional change that preceded the imperial settlement.

49–44 BC

Caesar’s Civil War

Julius Caesar defeated Pompey and became dictator before his assassination.

31 BC

Actium

Octavian defeated Antony and Cleopatra.

27 BC

Augustan Settlement

Octavian received the name Augustus and established the Principate.

43 AD

Roman conquest of Britain begins

Claudius launched the major imperial invasion of Britain.

68–69 AD

Year of the Four Emperors

Galba, Otho, Vitellius and Vespasian competed for power.

79 AD

Vesuvius eruption

Pompeii and Herculaneum were buried in the eruption.

117 AD

Trajanic maximum

The empire reached its greatest conventional territorial extent.

212 AD

Constitutio Antoniniana

Caracalla extended Roman citizenship to most free inhabitants of the empire.

235 AD

Crisis begins

Maximinus Thrax succeeded Severus Alexander amid military instability.

260 AD

Valerian captured

The Sasanian king Shapur I captured the emperor Valerian.

270–275 AD

Aurelian reunifies the empire

The Palmyrene and Gallic breakaways were defeated.

284 AD

Diocletian becomes emperor

The Tetrarchic transformation begins.

312 AD

Milvian Bridge

Constantine defeated Maxentius.

330 AD

Constantinople inaugurated

Constantine dedicated the new imperial capital.

378 AD

Adrianople

Valens was killed fighting the Goths.

395 AD

Theodosius dies

The imperial administration became permanently divided between eastern and western courts.

410 AD

Sack of Rome

Alaric and the Visigoths entered Rome.

455 AD

Vandal sack of Rome

Geiseric’s Vandals captured and plundered Rome.

476 AD

Deposition of Romulus Augustulus

The western imperial court in Italy ended; Julius Nepos continued as a claimant from Dalmatia.

527 AD

Justinian becomes emperor

The age of reconquest and Roman law codification begins.

537 AD

Hagia Sophia dedicated

Justinian’s rebuilt great church was inaugurated.

610 AD

Heraclius becomes emperor

A new phase of Roman–Persian war and later transformation begins.

717–718 AD

Siege of Constantinople

Leo III defended the capital against the Umayyad assault.

1071 AD

Manzikert

Roman forces suffered a major defeat against the Seljuks.

1204 AD

Fourth Crusade captures Constantinople

The Roman state fragmented into successor governments.

1261 AD

Constantinople recovered

Michael VIII restored Roman rule in the capital.

1453 AD

Fall of Constantinople

Constantine XI died during the Ottoman conquest; the Roman imperial state ended.
