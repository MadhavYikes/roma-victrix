Foundation and growth

Late western imperial capital and major Adriatic port.

Urban plan

Roman cities typically combined streets, public squares, temples, administrative buildings, baths, markets, entertainment venues and residential districts, but local plans varied considerably.

Government

Local councils, magistrates and wealthy households mediated between the city and provincial authorities.

Religion

Temples, shrines, priesthoods and later churches structured urban public space.

Economy

Markets, workshops, ports and roads connected Ravenna to regional and imperial networks.

Archaeology

Buildings, inscriptions, coins, pottery, sculpture and environmental evidence allow reconstruction of urban life.
