Roman Coinage

Money, portraiture and imperial communication. Coins circulated across the empire and are among the richest surviving sources for imperial titulature and political imagery.

Silver · Republic and early Empire

Denarius

A principal Roman silver denomination used for centuries.

Gold · Republic and Empire

Aureus

A high-value gold coin used for imperial payments and elite transactions.

Bronze · Empire

Sestertius

A large bronze denomination widely used in the imperial monetary system.

Orichalcum · Empire

Dupondius

A denomination commonly distinguished from the as by its size, weight and radiate portrait conventions.

Copper/bronze · Empire

As

A common lower denomination of the imperial monetary system.

Billon/silver alloy · Third century

Antoninianus

A radiate coin introduced under Caracalla and heavily debased during the crisis.

Gold · Late Empire and Eastern Roman

Solidus

A stable gold denomination introduced under Constantine and used for centuries.
